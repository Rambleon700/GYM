Progression Protocol

Single Tracked Metric
01

Track the Slot A opener only. DB Bench, DB RDL, DB Single-Arm Row, DB Goblet Squat — nothing else needs a number.

02

Bottom of range, top of range. Start at the lightest DB setting that's still RPE 7–8 for the low end of the rep window.

03

Add one rep per set, session to session, until every set hits the top of the range.

04

Then add load — next DB increment — and drop back to the bottom of the range.

05

At the 25kg ceiling, keep progressing with tempo (3-1-1), a pause at full stretch, or looping a band across the DB for added resistance — in that order.

06

Slots B and C get a ✅ / ❌ only — did reps hold or rise vs. last week. No load chasing needed there.

Reset window

Once you hit 25kg/hand

Tempo first: slow the eccentric to 3 seconds, pause 1 second at full stretch. This alone can add weeks of genuine progression at the same load.

Band overload next: loop a band around the DB and anchor it (under a foot for presses, under the bench for rows) so resistance increases through the range you're already strongest in.

Unilateral last: for any bilateral lift that's maxed out, switch to a single-arm/single-leg version of the same pattern — it re-opens the whole rep range at the same load.

Built for a 63-year-old lifter at 92kg bodyweight, equipped with adjustable dumbbells (25kg/hand), resistance bands, and a flat/incline bench. Week 6 is a mandatory deload — same movements, ~60% of working volume, RPE 6 — to bank recovery before the final push. Get medical clearance before starting if you have any cardiovascular, joint, or blood-pressure conditions.
